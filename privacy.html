What we collect

When you use the contact form, we receive the name, work email, company, message, and optional timing you enter. If you email us directly, we receive what you send.

How we use it

Only to reply to your inquiry and follow up on the conversation you started. We do not sell it, rent it, or add you to a mailing list.

Who processes it

  • Cloudflare hosts this site and receives the form submission.
  • Resend delivers the submission to our inbox by email.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

Retention and your choices

We keep inquiry emails for as long as they are useful to the conversation. To see or delete what you sent us, email contact@appelhaken.com.
