Supply chain and operations work that holds inside the operation

Appel Haken is a hands-on supply chain and operations consulting firm. Companies bring us in when the operation is underperforming or when growth has outpaced the system it runs on. We work from the data, and we stay until the fix holds.

The goal is not a deck. It is an operation leadership can trust.

What we do

We take on operational problems that are too urgent or too important to get wrong. Sometimes that means fixing what is broken. Sometimes it means building the operation from day 0.

Services

Hands-on execution and implementation

We build the tools and change the process ourselves. Then we stay until the result is real.

Turnaround and stabilization

When service has broken down or a key customer is losing confidence, we find the root cause and get the operation back under control.

Cost and performance

We find what is actually driving cost and fix the structure behind it. A one-time squeeze does not last.

Build and scale, from day 0

We design and stand up supply chains and operating models from the ground up. Growth breaks systems faster than most teams can redesign them.

Decisions from operating data

We build tools that turn fragmented operational data into a view leadership can act on. AI is sometimes part of the build. It is not the offer.

How we work

Every engagement follows the same sequence.

  1. Listen.

    We start with the people who feel the problem, inside the business and outside it.

  2. Diagnose.

    We pull the data and test whether it can be trusted. Then we find the real sources of cost and delay.

  3. Build.

    We build tools leadership will actually use. We redefine processes so performance does not depend on heroics.

  4. Stay.

    We stay involved through implementation until the operating result is delivered.

We often know where to act within the first 30 days. That is a pattern, not a promise.
