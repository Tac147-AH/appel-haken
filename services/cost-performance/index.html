Cost and performance improvements that survive the first fix

Savings last when you fix the structure behind the cost, not when you squeeze it once.

Why one-time cuts come back

A price squeeze shows up in one quarter. If the process and the sourcing model behind the cost have not changed, the cost comes back. We work on the structure so the result is still there after the first fix.

What we look at

  • Where the spend actually goes. Fragmented spend categories hide what you are paying. We standardize them so you can compare.
  • How you source. Sometimes the sourcing model is the problem, not the price. We restructure it when it is.
  • Price paid by part and supplier. When the same part costs different amounts from different suppliers, that gap is worth a conversation with the supply base.
  • The process that drives cost. Expediting and workarounds cost money every day. Fixing the process removes the cost at the source.

We will not quote a number before seeing your data. One example on this site shows a 12-37% spend reduction. Your result depends on where you start.

Is this just renegotiating with suppliers?

No. The work is changing how spend is organized and how sourcing is set up. Renegotiation is easier from there.

Will the savings hold?

That is the reason to fix the structure. We stay through implementation so the change is built into how the operation runs.
