About Brian Diehl

Brian Diehl is the Founder and Principal of Appel Haken, LLC, which he founded in 2023 after a career in engineering, procurement and operations leadership at Pitney Bowes.

Operating background

Before founding Appel Haken, Brian held four Vice President roles at Pitney Bowes:

  • Vice President, Procurement
  • Vice President, Global Manufacturing and Logistics
  • Regional Vice President, Parcel Operations
  • Vice President, Final Mile Development

At Pitney Bowes he led an organization of approximately 1,200 people. His responsibilities included $800M in global material and transportation spend and a $75M operating P&L.

He began his career as an electrical engineer, designing electronics and writing software for new products. That engineering foundation is why the firm builds its own analysis tools rather than handing over a deck.

Education

  • Bachelor of Science, Computer and Systems Engineering, Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI)
  • MBA, Finance, University of Connecticut
  • Graduate Certificate in Operational Excellence, Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)

How Appel Haken works

Appel Haken is built on a simple view: the recommendation only matters if it holds inside the operation. So the firm builds the tools and stays through implementation.

Brian spent years using data and automation to solve real operating problems and to make organizations more efficient and transparent. Appel Haken brings that same approach to companies of every size, without the overhead of a large consultancy.
