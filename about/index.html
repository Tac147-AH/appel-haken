About Brian Diehl

Brian Diehl is the Founder and Principal of Appel Haken, LLC, which was founded in 2023.

Operating background

Brian held four Vice President roles at Pitney Bowes, in this order:

  1. Vice President, Procurement
  2. Vice President, Global Manufacturing and Logistics
  3. Regional Vice President, Parcel Operations
  4. Vice President, Final Mile Development

Brian has also led an organization of approximately 1,200 employees.

Education

Brian has an engineering background as an RPI engineer. Brian earned a Graduate Certificate in Operational Excellence from MIT and holds an MBA.
