Frequently asked questions

Appel Haken is a hands-on supply chain and operations consulting firm. These are short answers to what people ask before they get in touch.

Questions

What is Appel Haken?

Appel Haken is a hands-on supply chain and operations consulting firm founded in 2023 by Brian Diehl. We fix operations that are underperforming and build ones that do not exist yet.

How is Appel Haken different from a typical consulting firm?

We execute instead of only advising. We build working tools and stay through implementation until the operating result is delivered.

Who do you work with?

Founders building a new operation, and operators or investors who need an existing operation to perform better.

What kinds of problems do you take on?

Five kinds: turnaround and stabilization, cost and performance, building operations from day 0, decisions from operating data, and hands-on execution of all of it.

How quickly will we see something useful?

We often identify where to act within the first 30 days. That is a pattern from past work, not a guarantee.

Is Appel Haken an AI firm?

No. Appel Haken is an operations firm. We build AI-enabled tools when they help leadership make an operating decision.

Are you a 3PL or a software vendor?

No. We design and improve operations. We do not move freight, and we do not sell software.

How do we start a conversation?

Email contact@appelhaken.com with a short description of what is breaking or what needs to be built. From there, we schedule a discovery call.
